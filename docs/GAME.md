# The game

You play solo or in teams of two. Every mission earns XP. The leaderboard is on the big screen.

## Missions

| Mission | Badge | XP |
|---|---|---|
| Tool Arena | Arena Judge | 100 for voting with a reason |
| 0 · First Launch | First Launch | 100 |
| 1 · Screens | Screen Smith | 100 per screen, 300 total |
| 2 · Login | Gatekeeper | Email 100 · Google 100 · Apple 100 |
| 3 · Save data | Vault | 300 |
| Boss Fight | Bug Slayer | 150 per bug, 450 total |
| 4 · Store-ready | Store-Ready | 300 |

## Bonuses

- **Fastest** to finish a mission: +50
- **Demo** on the big screen: +100
- **Helper**: get a stuck neighbour unstuck: +50

## Lifelines

- **Checkpoint**: jump to the next branch with `git checkout 02-login`. −100 XP, and you keep playing.
- **Peek**: look at the diff of the next branch. −50 XP.
- **Ask**: the facilitator helps once per mission. Free.
