# Daily — one-page brief

**One sentence:** Daily helps busy people keep three to five small habits by checking them off once a day.

**User:** a working professional in the Gulf who wants to build habits (read 10 pages, walk 8,000 steps, drink water) and checks the app once in the morning or at night.

## Screens

| Screen | What it shows | What the user can do |
|---|---|---|
| Today | Every habit, a check button, the current streak | Check or uncheck a habit for today, open a habit, add a habit |
| New habit | One name field | Save a habit (1–60 characters) |
| Habit detail | Name, current streak, last 7 days | Delete the habit after confirming |
| Sign in | Email, password, Google, Apple | Create an account or sign in |
| Settings | Signed-in email | Sign out, delete account |

## Data

- **Habit:** id, name, created date, owner
- **Check-in:** habit, day (local calendar day), owner

## Out of scope for version 1

Reminders and notifications, sharing, charts beyond 7 days, multiple check-ins per day.
